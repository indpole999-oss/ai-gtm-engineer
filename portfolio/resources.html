/ WHAT IS NOT SHIPPED

Honest verification boundaries.

Are these eight production workflows?

No. They are eight executable offline reference implementations. Live CRM, enrichment, email and calendar adapters need separate integration testing.

Does the interactive demo send emails or modify CRM?

No. It has no networking code. Even after sample approval, changes remain local to the browser session.

Is GAPS AI V2 production-ready?

Its development/staging functionality is not the same as a fully validated customer journey. Provider E2E, external side effects and acceptance evidence remain separate release gates.
