01 / ACCOUNT INTELLIGENCE

Account research that carries its sources.

A small reference implementation for turning account facts into an explainable decision, with evidence completeness as a first-class quality signal.

ETHICAL BOUNDARY

No real contacts, client data, paid providers or external actions.

The business problem

Revenue teams often start with a company name and end with an outreach message without ever checking whether the research actually supports the claims. The right engineering question is: which evidence supported the decision to contact this account?

What I implemented

The offline sample includes three fictional organizations. Each has an industry, employee count, optional buying signal, and source-tagged facts. These inputs are processed through a deterministic score rather than an opaque LLM assertion.

Observed output on fixtures

The fictional SaaS and Healthtech accounts each score 100/100 with complete provided evidence; the Transport account scores 0/100. Removing one of two source facts from a complete account changes its score from 100 to 85. This illustrates sensitivity to evidence completeness, not predictive sales accuracy.

Trade-off and failure lesson

Deterministic weights are transparent and easy to test, but the scoring policy is hand-designed and has no validated correlation to revenue outcomes. A production account research system also needs timestamps, factual corroboration, stale-data handling, uncertainty labels, and quality sampling.
