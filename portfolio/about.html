/ WHY THIS PATH

From outbound operations to GTM engineering.

While supporting U.S. technology accounts, I repeatedly saw the same friction: account research in one tool, contacts in another, qualification in spreadsheets, activity in CRM, and outreach outside the system of record.

The interesting engineering question isn't “Can an AI model write an email?” It's whether a workflow can carry useful, verifiable context through the whole process without duplication, invented facts, or accidental external action.

My independent GAPS AI project explores that problem with research, AI workflows, approval controls, system-of-record integration architecture, and a customer-facing interface. The work is still evolving, and I believe explaining what's incomplete is part of engineering credibility.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

Evidence before automation.Human approval before external action.Durable state before success claims.Clear documentation before handoff.

These principles govern the portfolio's test-only workflow examples.
